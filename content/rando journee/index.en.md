Whether you're just passing through Isère or a year-round Grenoble resident, come discover or rediscover Chartreuse, Vercors, and Belledonne. I guide you on the trails, sharing my passion for the mountain environment.

Here are some example hikes I can accompany you on. Many other routes are possible, and I adapt the project according to your desires and requests. Don't hesitate to [contact me]({{< relref "/contact" >}}) to choose a date and destination together!

## Vercors

### Les Balcons du Vercors

A hike at the foot of the majestic cliffs of Vercors, offering a panoramic view of the Ecrins.

- Duration: one day
- Difficulty: moderate to difficult (evolving in rocky terrain)
- Audience: adults and children from 12 years old - maximum 12 people
- Location: departure from Col de l'Arzelier
- Dates: on request
- Rate for a constituted group: €250

### Rocher du Baconnet

Little frequented, the Rocher du Baconnet offers an unparalleled view of Trièves, the cliffs of Vercors, and the mythical Mont Aiguille.

- Duration: one day
- Difficulty: easy to moderate
- Audience: adults and children from 12 years old - maximum 12 people
- Location: departure from Gresse-en-Vercors
- Dates: on request
- Rate for a constituted group: €250

## Chartreuse

### Tour du Vallon d'Hurtières

A very beautiful beech forest opens and closes this route, which takes you through the open landscapes of the Hurtières valley, at the foot of the limestone cliff of the Grande Sure. Clear weather will even allow you to enjoy a view of Mont Blanc.

- Duration: one day
- Difficulty: moderate
- Audience: adults and children from 12 years old - maximum 12 people
- Location: departure from Col de la Charmette
- Dates: on request
- Rate for a constituted group: €250

## Belledonne

### Le Grand Van from Chamrousse

Chamrousse is a privileged gateway to Belledonne: the ski resort quickly gives way to preserved atmospheres. This loop takes you to the must-see Lacs Roberts, then allows you to reach the summit of Grand Van via wild and unmarked paths.

- Duration: one day
- Difficulty: difficult (evolving in rocky terrain on unmarked paths)
- Audience: adults and teens from 14 years old - maximum 12 people
- Location: departure from Chamrousse
- Dates: on request
- Rate for a constituted group: €250

### Tour du Grand Replomb

The Tour du Grand Replomb offers a great variety of landscapes, typical of the Belledonne massif: high-altitude forest, mineral atmosphere characteristic of the Col de la Mine de Fer, and a glacial lake with a commanding view of Lac de Crop.

- Duration: one day
- Difficulty: difficult (evolving in rocky terrain, significant elevation gain)
- Audience: adults and teens from 14 years old - maximum 12 people
- Location: departure from Pré Marcel
- Dates: on request
- Rate for a constituted group: €250
