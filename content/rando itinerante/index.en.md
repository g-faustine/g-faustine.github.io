Multi-day trekking is an exceptional experience!
Sleeping in the mountains means completely disconnecting from your daily routine and refocusing on your needs, sensations, and the pleasures of the moment.
By walking for several days, it is also possible to access more remote places: trekking then becomes a micro-adventure and an immersion in the wildest environments. Mountain huts allow you to find a comfortable and cozy space in a convivial atmosphere.

I offer multi-day stays with overnight stays in huts around Grenoble. These stays are adaptable according to your desires and requests. [Contact me]({{< relref "/contact" >}}) to choose a date and destination together!
