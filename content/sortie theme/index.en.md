## Botanical Hikes

### Curious Plants

Take a walk and open your eyes to the plants along the path: many of them hold surprises, whether they are edible, medicinal, or deadly poisonous. During this outing, I will share anecdotes about the plants we encounter. Enough to awaken your curiosity!

- Duration: half-day
- Difficulty: very easy
- Audience: adults and children from 12 years old, maximum 10 people
- Dates: on request, from April to August
- Location: around Grenoble, depending on the season (vegetation progress) and your preferences
- Rate for a constituted group: €150

### Budding Botanist

What exactly is a flower? How does it work? How to recognize plants for sure?
During a mountain hike, we will learn to observe and describe a plant, then discover the basics of identifying plant families using a determination key. We will alternate between discussion times, practical workshops, and walking.

The goal of this outing is twofold: to give you basic methods for recognizing plant species, but above all, to invite you to observe the detail and the incredible diversity of the plant world.

- Duration: one day
- Difficulty: easy
- Audience: adults, maximum 10 people
- Location: around Grenoble, depending on the season (vegetation progress) and your preferences
- Dates: on request, from April to August
- Rate for a constituted group: €250

[Contact me]({{< relref "/contact" >}}) to choose a date and destination together!

## Ornithology

### Listening to Birds

It's spring, the birds are singing... But have you ever wondered who these virtuosic singers are?
For the duration of a mountain hike, let's open our ears wide to be charmed by the birdsong and learn to recognize the main species. It is often easier to hear the birds than to see them!

- Duration: one day
- Difficulty: easy
- Audience: adults, maximum 10 people
- Location: according to your preferences and the season, around Grenoble
- Dates: on request, from April to August
- Rate for a constituted group: €250

[Contact me]({{< relref "/contact" >}}) to choose a date and destination together!
